Game Story & Objective

Your mission is to explore Treasure Island, survive encounters with dangerous creatures (fire dragons, giant cyclopes, and wild rivers), and make the right choices to discover the ultimate hidden treasure!
---
Features

- **Rich Visuals:** Detailed ASCII art for the island map, dragon, cyclops, and end-game scenarios.
- **Robust Input Handling:** Case-insensitive inputs (`LEFT`/`left`, `SWIM`/`swim`, etc.) with Turkish/English character handling (`RİGHT`/`RIGHT`).
- **Branching Storyline:** Multiple pathways leading to different game-over scenarios or a victorious finish.
- **Nested Loops:** Ensures players provide valid options without crashing or abruptly ending the game.
